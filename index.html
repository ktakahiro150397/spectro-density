How it works: The analyzer detects the three strongest low-frequency peaks (typically vocal fundamentals and lower formants), sums their power, then sums the power of peaks in the 2 kHz – 6 kHz range (singer's formant / brilliance zone). The ratio = Low Power / High Power. A ratio > 1 means the voice is warm/chest-dominant; ratio near 1 is balanced; ratio < 1 means the high-frequency brilliance is dominant.
